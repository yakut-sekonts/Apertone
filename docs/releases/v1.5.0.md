# Apertone 1.5.0

Apertone 1.5.0 adds adaptive editing tools that remain editable, local and
non-destructive. Match Look transfers a bounded tone and color treatment from
selected references, while mask refinements and detailed face selections extend
the existing local-adjustment document without changing originals.

## Match Look

- Build a self-contained Quick Look layer from 1–16 selected reference photos.
- Transfer supported tone and color controls without linking the result to the
  references after creation.
- Preserve Exposure and White Balance by default, or include them explicitly.
- Adjust Amount from 0–200%, enable, reorder or remove the resulting layer.
- Exclude geometry, masks, retouching, AI-derived data and sampled Point Color
  ranges from transfer.

## Mask refinement and reuse

- Apply persistent Edge shift and Edge feather to bitmap-backed selections.
- Share the same refined result across preview, overlay, thumbnails and export.
- Duplicate a combined selection with its ordered Add, Subtract and Intersect
  operands. Copies receive fresh identifiers and remain independently editable.

## Detailed face selections

- Select Face, Eyes or Lips locally through Apple Vision face landmarks.
- Click the intended face in group photos before publishing the selection.
- Refuse incomplete or low-confidence landmarks rather than creating an
  approximate mask.

Skin, hair, teeth and clothing selections are not included because the current
local provider does not expose reliable semantic regions for them.

## Install

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. Download
`Apertone-1.5.0-macos-arm64.dmg`, drag Apertone to Applications and open it.
The open-source build is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized. If Gatekeeper
blocks the first launch, Control-click Apertone, choose Open, then confirm Open.

Existing installations receive 1.5.0 through the update bell. The release also
provides the Sparkle-signed ZIP, `appcast.xml`, SHA-256 manifest and build
provenance.
