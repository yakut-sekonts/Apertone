## Apertone 3.1.0

Library and Develop now share a workspace on macOS and Windows. Switch modules
in the upper-right corner. The filmstrip runs along the bottom, with the
filename and status directly above it. Both Day and Night appearances are
available.

### Review and edit in Library

- Browse the catalog and collections below the Navigator on the left. In
  Loupe, the Navigator and RGB histogram follow the active photo. Rate or
  flag that photo below the image, even when several photos are selected.
- Use Quick Develop below the histogram on the right to apply a preset,
  adjust exposure by one third of a stop, or restore as-shot white balance.
  Exposure adjustments start from each selected photo’s own value. Undo and
  Redo apply to the whole batch.

### Compare as you work in Develop

- Browse presets below the Navigator on the left. The right panel starts with
  the histogram, Crop, Retouch, Masking and Basic adjustments.
- Press **Y** for side-by-side Before and After on the canvas. Edit the right
  image while keeping the original in view. Use the Navigator for linked zoom
  and pan, or select **Split comparison** for a draggable divider. Press **Y**
  or **Escape** to return to the edited photo.
- On smaller displays, scroll the side panels to reach additional controls,
  or toggle them with **F7** and **F8**. **F6** toggles the filmstrip.

### Work in a narrow Windows window

- Press **F7** for the left panel or **F8** for the right panel. Opening one
  closes the other. Press its key again to return to the photo.
- In Library, **Import** and **Export** sit together in the bottom bar when
  the left panel is hidden.
- Press **F11** to enter full screen. Press it again to restore the window
  to its previous size or maximized state.

### Install or update

- **Windows 10 or 11 x64.** Use the bell in Apertone to check for updates.
  For a fresh installation, extract `Apertone-3.1.0-windows-x64.zip` into
  a folder and launch `Apertone.exe` from that folder. Local AI inference
  uses the [Microsoft Visual C++ x64 Redistributable](https://learn.microsoft.com/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist).
- **macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon.** Use the bell to update through
  Sparkle. For a fresh installation, open `Apertone-3.1.0-macos-arm64.dmg`
  and drag Apertone to Applications. See the [first-launch guide](https://github.com/yakut-sekonts/Apertone/blob/v3.1.0/docs/OPEN_SOURCE_RELEASE.md).
- Verify macOS downloads with `SHA256SUMS` and the Windows ZIP with
  `SHA256SUMS-windows.txt` in this release.
